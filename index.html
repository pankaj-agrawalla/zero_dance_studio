About Us

Where Every Step Tells a Story

Zer0 Dance Studio is Pune's home for dancers of every age and level, founded and directed by Adesh (@adeshaddy). From energetic kids' batches to empowering ladies-only sessions, we build confidence, fitness and pure joy through movement.

With studios in Mahalunge and Baner/Balewadi, we bring professional choreography and a fun, judgment-free environment closer to you — whether you're prepping for a sangeet performance, chasing a fitness goal, or discovering dance for the very first time.

  • Professional choreographers & personalised attention
  • Batches for kids (age 4–13), teens, adults & ladies-only groups
  • Sangeet, wedding, commercial & corporate event choreography
  • Clean, spacious, mirror-walled studios with sound systems
